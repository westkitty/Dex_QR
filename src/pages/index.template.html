Dex_QR

Offline file transfer over an animated QR fountain stream

Open the sender on the device that has the file, and the receiver on the device with the camera.

Both pages are single self-contained HTML files. Save them and open them from disk (file://); they make no network requests (a Content-Security-Policy blocks all connections).

Real phone and laptop camera scanning has not been tested by the author yet. See docs/TEST_CHECKLIST.md in the repository.
