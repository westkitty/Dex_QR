You can enter it while scanning: it is checked against the sender's key-check frame within seconds, and scanning continues either way.
Camera, decoder and file input
“Decoded” counts independent packets (rank) out of the K needed; the file appears once it reaches K. Duplicate frames and frames from another transfer are ignored. Stopping the camera keeps what you have; Reset discards it (it asks first). Camera scanning on real phones and laptops has not been tested by the author — see docs/TEST_CHECKLIST.md.
