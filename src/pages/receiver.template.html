“Decoded” counts independent packets (rank) out of the K needed; the file appears once it reaches K. The squares above fill in the same proportion; with small files the decoder solves every block at once at the end, so a square stands for a share of the information, not a specific byte range. Duplicate frames and frames from another transfer are ignored.
Stopping the camera keeps what you have; Reset discards it (it asks first). Camera scanning on real phones and laptops has not been tested by the author — see docs/TEST_CHECKLIST.md.
