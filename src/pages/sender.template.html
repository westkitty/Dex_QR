Dex_QR sender

Offline file transfer as an animated QR fountain stream. Nothing leaves this page; no network is used.
Anyone who can see or record the screen can collect the encrypted stream and guess passphrases offline as fast as their hardware allows. Use a long random passphrase (e.g. 5+ random words) and share it out of band. Without a passphrase, anyone who sees enough frames gets the file.
